How to actually get good at this

Step 1 15 minutes a day One Warm-Up Set daily beats a three hour cram the night before. Your brain files facts while you sleep.
Step 2 Chase the red Wrong answers are the whole point. Run Fix My Mistakes until that list is empty.
Step 3 Say it out loud Explain photosynthesis to a parent, a mirror, or your camera. If you can teach it, you know it.
Step 4 Learn the units Metric conversions and lab tools show up on every single tryout test and they are free points.
