⚡ Math Sprint

Answer as many as you can before time runs out. Harder questions are worth more.

Ready to sprint?

  • You have 10 minutes. Type just the number and press Enter.
  • Before each question, pick Easy (5 pts), Medium (30 pts) or Hard (80 pts). Answering fast adds a small speed bonus.
  • Spend your points on upgrades: a Multiplier (every answer is worth more) and a Brain bonus (extra points on Medium and Hard). Spend early so they pay off longer!
  • Tip: rushing Easy questions won't win. Getting Medium and Hard ones right is worth much more.
  • Easy is one quick idea. Medium is two small steps. Hard needs a contest trick. Pick what you think you can get right!
  • A wrong answer scores 0 and shows you the right answer. Skipping costs 5 points.
  • When time is up, the points you have left are your score. You get a code to paste in the class chat.
🚫🤖 Please don't use AI or a calculator. Do the math yourself. It's the only way to get better.
